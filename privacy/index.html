Privacy Policy

Frenzy Devs · Last updated: August 29, 2026

Summary: We respect your privacy. Frenzy Devs does not sell personal data. We collect only the minimal information necessary to provide, secure, and improve our applications and services.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Frenzy Devs ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our website, mobile applications, or related digital services. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver and improve our services:

  • Account Details: Username, email address, and authentication information when you register.
  • Usage & Performance Data: Anonymous diagnostic information, device type, operating system, and crash logs.
  • Communications: Messages and details provided when contacting our support team.
  • Anonymized Metrics: Aggregate usage patterns to optimize user experience without accessing private communications or files.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data strictly to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve our software and services.
  • Respond to user support requests and questions.
  • Monitor system performance, fix bugs, and prevent abuse or security threats.
  • Deliver necessary service notifications and updates.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is only shared under specific circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted hosting and cloud infrastructure partners under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law or valid legal processes.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of company assets.

5. Data Security & Retention

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and secure access controls to protect your data. We retain information only for as long as needed to fulfill operational and legal purposes.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt out of optional analytics tracking through app settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data requests, please contact us:

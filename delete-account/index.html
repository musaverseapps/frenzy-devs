Delete Your Ascend Account

Account & data deletion request
ASCEND // ACCOUNT DELETION

If you would like to delete your Ascend account and associated data, you can submit a deletion request to us.

Send an email to:

musaverseapps@gmail.com

Please use the subject:

Delete my Ascend account

What to include

To help us locate your account, please include the email address associated with your Ascend account.

You do not need to send your password or any other authentication credentials.

What happens next?

  1. We receive your account deletion request.
  2. We verify the request and locate the associated account.
  3. Your Ascend account and associated user data are deleted in accordance with our privacy policy.
  4. We confirm the completion of the deletion request.
Please note:

Account deletion may permanently remove your notes, todos, account information, and other data associated with your Ascend account.

Deletion cannot be undone once completed.

Need help?

If you are having trouble accessing your account or have questions about your data, contact us using the email above.

For more information about how Ascend handles data, see our Privacy Policy.
